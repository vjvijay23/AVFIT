Concept 1: The Nexus Infinite Loop

The Unbroken Continuum of Fitness & Community

Combines an ascending apex ('A') and a grounded cradle ('V') into an infinity loop. Symbolizes the complete health loop: track your steps, scan your nutrition, crush your home workout, and celebrate with the community.

🔍 Comparative Breakdown of the 4 New Designs

1. The Nexus Infinite Most Balanced

Ultra-sleek ribbon loop. Instantly recognizable at tiny favicon sizes and massive billboards alike. Tells the story of an unbroken healthy lifestyle.

2. The Forward Velocity Most Athletic

Dual forward-raking aerodynamic blades. Radiates speed, pace, and forward drive. Perfect for running tracking, leaderboards, and GPS challenges.

3. Monogram Ligature Most Premium

Minimalist typographic mark where 'A' and 'V' fuse into a continuous architectural ribbon with negative space. High-end lifestyle brand aesthetic (like Alo / On Running).

4. Peak Vitality Most Holistic

Harmonizes the two pillars of health: a heart silhouette (cardio, wellness, food nutrition) enclosing a mountain peak (strength, personal endurance).
